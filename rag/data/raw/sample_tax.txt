Section 80C allows taxpayers to claim deductions up to ₹1.5 Lakh per financial year for investments in Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana (SSY), PPF, and ELSS.
Under Section 80D, deduction up to ₹25,000 is allowed for medical insurance premiums for self and family. Senior citizens can claim up to ₹50,000.
The PM Mudra Loan scheme provides micro-loans up to ₹10 Lakh for non-corporate, non-farm small/micro enterprises.
